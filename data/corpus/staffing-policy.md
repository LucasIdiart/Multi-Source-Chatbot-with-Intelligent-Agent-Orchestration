# Staffing and Contractor Assignment Policy

## Allocation limits
Nobody may be allocated above their contracted availability. Full-time employees have 100% availability.
Contractors may have a lower contracted availability (for example 60% or 80%); the assignment tool
rejects any allocation that would push a person above it. Allocations are expressed as a percentage
of a full working week, between 1% and 100% per project.

## Matching skills to projects
When choosing someone for a project, prefer people whose skills cover the project's required skills
with a level of 4 or higher. Level 5 means the person can lead the topic; level 3 means they can
contribute with review.

## Regulated clients
Engagements for healthcare and fintech clients (currently Aurora Health and Cedar Bank) must include
at least one person with the compliance skill. Contractors working on regulated engagements must sign
the client's data processing addendum before their first day, which the kickoff workflow requests
automatically.

## Contractor rates
Contractor hourly rates above 100 USD require approval from the engineering manager (Ana Ruiz) before
the assignment is confirmed. The rate is recorded in the person's profile.

## Removing someone from a project
Use the offboarding workflow. It revokes repository and cloud access within 24 hours and asks the
delivery lead for a handover note.
