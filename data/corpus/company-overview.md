# Northwind Labs — Company Overview

## Who we are
Northwind Labs is a 12-person software consultancy that builds data, cloud and product engineering
solutions for mid-size companies. We mix full-time employees with vetted independent contractors so
that every engagement gets the exact skill set it needs.

## How delivery works
Every client project has a delivery lead who owns scope, staffing and status reporting. Projects move
through four states: planned, active, on hold and closed. Work is tracked as tasks with the statuses
todo, in progress, blocked and done.

## Current clients
We currently serve Aurora Health (healthcare), Bluefin Logistics (logistics), Cedar Bank (fintech)
and Driftwood Retail (retail). Cedar Bank is our largest account, with two engagements: the Cloud
Migration and the upcoming Fraud Detection Engine.

## Tools
Staffing changes, task updates and workflow triggers are executed through the PM Assistant, which
records every change in an auditable action log.
