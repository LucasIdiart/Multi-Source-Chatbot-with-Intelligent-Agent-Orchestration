# Project Brief — Mobile Checkout (PRJ-04)

## Goal
Native one-tap checkout for Driftwood Retail's Android and iOS apps, aiming to cut checkout abandonment
by 15%.

## Why it is on hold
In September 2026 Driftwood Retail paused the project while it chooses between two payment providers.
The Android prototype is blocked until the provider SDK is known. The client expects a decision by the
end of October 2026.

## Restart plan
When the project restarts, trigger the kickoff workflow again, raise the mobile engineer's allocation
and add a UX designer for the iOS flow.
